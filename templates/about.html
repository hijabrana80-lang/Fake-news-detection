About the Project

AI-Powered Fake News Detection and Source Traceability Using Blockchain

TRUTHLINE is the working interface of that project: a digital fact-checking desk where a machine-learning model reads a story and a tamper-evident ledger preserves the record of every verdict it produces.

{{ section_heading("Purpose", kicker="Why this exists") }}

Misinformation spreads faster than it can be corrected, and most corrections carry no evidence that they were checked at all. This project combines two complementary mechanisms:

  • {{ icon('spark', 16) }}A detection model that classifies submitted text as FAKE or REAL using linguistic patterns learned from labelled news articles.
  • {{ icon('chain', 16) }}A traceability ledger that records each verdict as a SHA-256 fingerprint in a hash chain, optionally anchored to an Ethereum-compatible network.
  • {{ icon('search', 16) }}A verification desk that lets any visitor re-check a stored record and confirm that nothing has been altered.

{{ icon('shield', 16) }} The system reports model confidence and record integrity. It never claims that a story is objectively true or false.

{{ section_heading("Honest limitations", kicker="Read before citing") }}

Dataset

Trained on 1,991 curated articles from the ISOT Fake/True corpus. That is a small dataset relative to production fact-checking systems.

Optimistic accuracy

Reported hold-out accuracy is high partly because Reuters articles in the corpus carry a (Reuters) marker. Results on out-of-domain text will be lower.

Prediction, not proof

The classifier estimates how similar text is to its training examples. It cannot verify facts, sources or intent.
