How it works

  1. Preprocess — strip URLs, HTML, noise; normalise text.
  2. Vectorise — TF-IDF over unigrams & bigrams plus style statistics (caps, exclamation, clickbait terms).
  3. Classify — Logistic Regression returns FAKE / REAL with a confidence score.
  4. Trace — SHA-256 of the article is chained locally and anchored on chain when available.
