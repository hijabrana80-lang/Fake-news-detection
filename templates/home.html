{# ------------------------------------------------------------------ #} {# How it works — six-stage pipeline #} {# ------------------------------------------------------------------ #}
{{ section_heading("How the system works", kicker="Six-stage pipeline") }}

01 — Submit

Paste the story

A headline or full article enters the analysis workspace.

02 — Analyze

AI reads the language

Text is normalised, vectorised and scored against the trained model.

03 — Predict

Fake or real, with a score

The classifier returns a label and the probability behind it.

04 — Hash

Fingerprint the text

SHA-256 turns the article into a permanent content fingerprint.

05 — Record

Write the ledger entry

The verdict is chained to the previous record — anchored on-chain when available.

06 — Verify

Trace the source

Anyone can re-check the record later and confirm nothing changed.
